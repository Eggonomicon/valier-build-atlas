# CP01 · Planted diagonal facade bay

Recessed two-level glazing, pale diagonal knees, a planted sill and a four-wide flush entry. Repeat the bay every 16 blocks along the hall.

Local +X is east and +Z is south. Heights are relative to the piece origin. The walk of a level piece is Y0. A port lists X along a north or south face and Z along an east or west face.

| Opening | Face | Along | Walking Y |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Street-side door | E | 6–9 | 0 |
| Hall-side threshold | W | 6–9 | 0 |

Choose a world position for local (0,0,0). Add **(0, -2, 0)** to that position for the clipboard minimum. The occupied source coordinates end at **(9, 17, 15)** inclusive. Both WorldEdit origin and offset are zero.

Place the east edge next to the west sidewalk. Center the hall opening on Z6–9 and continue its floor west at Y0. The native diagonals continue above the planted sill.



Open `index.html` for level grids and `coordinates.csv.gz` for precise hosts. The grid keys give material metadata, shaped-block facing and multipart slots. Stock counts placed blocks and separate cut parts. Fill any marked sources by hand after closing the bank. Use the matching facing file in Schematica with Printer OFF for manual placement.
