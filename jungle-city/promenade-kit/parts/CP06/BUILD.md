# CP06 · Faceted avenue bend

The avenue shifts four blocks east over twenty rows. Its flat curb follows the bend in one-block facets and both twelve-wide roads meet flush.

Local +X is east and +Z is south. Heights are relative to the piece origin. The walk of a level piece is Y0. A port lists X along a north or south face and Z along an east or west face.

| Opening | Face | Along | Walking Y |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Road | N | 0–11 | 0 |
| Road | S | 4–15 | 0 |

Choose a world position for local (0,0,0). Add **(0, -2, 0)** to that position for the clipboard minimum. The occupied source coordinates end at **(15, -1, 19)** inclusive. Both WorldEdit origin and offset are zero.
