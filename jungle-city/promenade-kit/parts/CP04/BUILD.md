# CP04 · Glazed crossing span

A continuous six-wide crossing enclosed by recessed blue glass. Pale molded coping, dark mullions and pin lights repeat every six blocks.

Local +X is east and +Z is south. Heights are relative to the piece origin. The walk of a level piece is Y0. A port lists X along a north or south face and Z along an east or west face.

| Opening | Face | Along | Walking Y |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| West landing | W | 1–6 | 0 |
| East landing | E | 1–6 | 0 |

Choose a world position for local (0,0,0). Add **(0, -2, 0)** to that position for the clipboard minimum. The occupied source coordinates end at **(23, 4, 7)** inclusive. Both WorldEdit origin and offset are zero.

Match the two landings at the same walk height. Place CP10 at (+6, 0, 0) beneath each surveyed span or use a bank abutment where the span ends. Skip or relocate a bearing that conflicts with a road or channel.



Open `index.html` for level grids and `coordinates.csv.gz` for precise hosts. The grid keys give material metadata, shaped-block facing and multipart slots. Stock counts placed blocks and separate cut parts. Fill any marked sources by hand after closing the bank. Use the matching facing file in Schematica with Printer OFF for manual placement.
