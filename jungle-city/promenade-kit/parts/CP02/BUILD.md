# CP02 · Slim promenade lantern

Round charcoal mast on a cyan-capped plinth, with a white inset diffuser and a thin pale crown. Place it on the outer sidewalk at regular intervals.

Local +X is east and +Z is south. Heights are relative to the piece origin. The walk of a level piece is Y0. A port lists X along a north or south face and Z along an east or west face.

Choose a world position for local (0,0,0). Add **(0, -1, 0)** to that position for the clipboard minimum. The occupied source coordinates end at **(0, 5, 0)** inclusive. Both WorldEdit origin and offset are zero.

Replace the existing paving host at Y−1 with the matched paving block or leave it in place; set the fixture at that same finished height. Keep lamps outside clear door and walkway ports.
