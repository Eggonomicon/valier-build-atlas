# CP08 · Planted pocket court

A river terrace with two raised gardens, alliums, jungle trees and shallow bench seats. The four-wide crosswalk opens from the avenue onto a clear overlook and the six-wide path continues past it.

Local +X is east and +Z is south. Heights are relative to the piece origin. The walk of a level piece is Y0. A port lists X along a north or south face and Z along an east or west face.

Choose a world position for local (0,0,0). Add **(0, -2, 0)** to that position for the clipboard minimum. The occupied source coordinates end at **(15, 6, 15)** inclusive. Both WorldEdit origin and offset are zero.
