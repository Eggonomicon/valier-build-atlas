# CP05 · Straight cliff avenue

A six-wide charcoal tiled lane with pale curb lines and a flush sidewalk on each side. Set the hall against the west edge and the river walk against the east edge.

Local +X is east and +Z is south. Heights are relative to the piece origin. The walk of a level piece is Y0. A port lists X along a north or south face and Z along an east or west face.

Choose a world position for local (0,0,0). Add **(0, -2, 0)** to that position for the clipboard minimum. The occupied source coordinates end at **(11, -1, 15)** inclusive. Both WorldEdit origin and offset are zero.
