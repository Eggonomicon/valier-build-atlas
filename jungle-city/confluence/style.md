## Lay out the banks before the buildings

1. Mark the actual waterline, solid banks and two candidate bridge landings in the PDIM. Look back from the opposite bank. Leave a strip of exposed clay above the main windows and a sightline along the water toward the next bend.
2. Set the lower street on a ledge. Use the 12-wide road section with a six-block charcoal carriageway and a three-block gray sidewalk on either side. Carry the building-side paving through each doorway. Turn the stream-side sidewalk out onto a terrace at a landing. Keep rail ends and planters clear of the opening.
3. Cut the portal into a shoulder of the mesa. Use the 14-wide MK14 or MK15 portal around the 12-wide road and carry the paving through. Show white clay outside its pale molded band and a dark reveal on the inside. Let the jungle continue over its crown.
4. Place the glazed footbridge on a higher ledge with level approaches on both banks. MK35 and MK36 have an eight-block envelope, six clear walking blocks and four blocks of interior headroom. Align the bridge cornice with a hall ledge where the span meets a facade. Collect the ribs into set-back bearings so the water stays visible below. Size the leg down to surveyed solid material.
5. Set the public path on the bank between the hall and the water. Use MK26 or MK27 for a six-clear promenade. At a park entrance, center the four-wide opening of MK32 on the walk and run the same threshold paving into the court. A short MK37 or MK38 flight changes level by four blocks over 16. Give the flight a level landing before a turn or doorway.
6. Keep the natural stream continuous where it passes the buildings. Place trees on wider bank shelves and lower flowers near overlook rails. For a constructed water court, set the 12-square MK33 basin beside the path, build its rim and bed, then fill its 60 marked water positions by hand. Leave an unbroken dry approach past the water feature.

## Check it from three viewpoints

- **From the opposite bank**, confirm that the bridge crosses daylight, white clay shows above the windows, and the height of the crown remains legible behind the roofline.
- **From the hall door**, walk to the waterfront or park. The paving should meet at each threshold and the rail should stop at the turn. Look along the road to check that the portal frames the next stretch.
- **From the water**, look back under the span. If a solid wall fills the view, set the next bearing back into the bank. If the rail and lights conceal the glazing, shorten the lit strip and move the tree to the next ledge.

All directions refer to local coordinates with +X east and +Z south. Set walking heights relative to the selected module's floor before converting to world Y. Survey the target PDIM for terrain, bearings and water. Use the layer drawings and native host data for each block and cut.
