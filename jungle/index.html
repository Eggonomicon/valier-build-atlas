07 / PREPARE THE ALTERNATE SITE
Build a material sample first. Survey your PDIM next.
  1. Gather a small palette test: Korp14, Agon0/13/15, light-blue clear glass, Caelestis cyan/white and the native shaped details. Build SD01 and a facade bay from the existing refined guide; compare in your shader.
  2. Once the PDIM exists, measure its river, ridge tops, ground and intended access. Transfer the relationships—low avenue, cliff hall, high crossing, open conservatory—to that terrain. Keep the seed424242 coordinates as this example only.
  3. Stake the four envelopes and walking levels from the plan. Inspect rock above and behind them. Reserve maintenance and smoke/vent clearances before excavation.
  4. Build foundations and the pedestrian route in short inspected sections. Finish railings, abutments, retaining walls, portal weathering and gallery access for the actual terrain before treating infrastructure as ready.
  5. Use the preserved building source guides for the block details. Install machines, boilers and services manually after a separate terrain-specific engineering pass. The current EV network belongs to Sample A.
This completes the alternate sample’s measured terrain, material selection and coordinate architecture/civil layout. It does not claim a fully adapted81-building B city, a commissioned plant, an in-game schematic test or a construction-ready terrain/support design.
