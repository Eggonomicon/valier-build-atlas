# Valier / Steam through EV field guide

Coordinates are city-local: northwest origin, +X east, +Z south, civilY0=future surveyed surfaceS. No world anchor or rotation is assigned. Core864, civil876 and HQ405..469 are preserved. This is a coordinate/configuration construction plan with static checks, not an operating factory or a quest-completion claim.

## Survival building and schematic contents

The separate architecture-only ZIP contains27 unchanged refined shell/component schematics. No machines, tanks, coils, pipes, cables or conduits are included. Use Schematica's ghost render with Printer OFF and manually place your own materials. Machine/utility positions and exact floor/cap/landing removals are separate CSVs, grids and configuration targets. Do not use WorldEdit paste for manual survival construction. Architectural shape tile entities are retained because the facade uses ArchitectureCraft and microblock cuts.

## Counting and limitations

Per-stage stock is the installed item inventory for that snapshot. It is not a recipe-ingredient bill or incremental shopping list. stage-changes.csv separately identifies additions/replacements; recovered items are not deducted. Reinforced door upper halves consume no separate door item. Source-shell blocks are not counted again. Exact removals are separate. Temporary supports, tools, battery chemistry, fuel/water/cells and external raw inputs are separate requirements.

All process stations use manual inventory/fluid-cell handling unless explicitly noted for the refinery or dedicated fuel/water networks. Recipe-specific ore byproducts, gases/acids, solder/circuit settings and cleanroom requirements remain distinct. LCR-only recipes are not supported merely by an EV singleblock reactor. IV and later departments remain preserved reservations. Actual crafting recipes, formation, throughput, target-game collision/lighting, pollution controls and native import/re-import remain open. No functional machine schematic is exported.
