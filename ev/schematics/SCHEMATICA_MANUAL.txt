VALIER / THROUGH EV / ARCHITECTURE ONLY

These27 schematics contain building fabric, shaped framing, microblock details,
paving, architectural lights and planting. They contain no machines, tanks,
coils, pipes, cables or conduits. The only tile entities are ArchitectureCraft
shapes and ForgeMultipart cut architectural materials. Those tile entities are
required for the smooth facade details and do not provide machinery.

For survival/manual building:
1. Put the .schematic files in .minecraft/schematics (Schematica's directory).
2. Use Controls > Schematica > Load Schematic with your chosen key binding.
3. Select a file and enable rendering. Position its minimum corner from its
   source shell origin; survey the real PDIM before choosing world coordinates.
4. Keep Printer OFF. The ghost display is a placement reference. Gather and
   place every block yourself. Do not use WorldEdit paste for manual survival.
5. ArchitectureCraft/microblock preview support may vary; use the preserved
   layer/shape/material guide for manual orientation. Target-game ghost display
   and re-import have not been certified.

Steam Hall01-01 and all numbered halls use their existing shell origins and
Y-7 lower bounds. Generic hall X/Z minima are22+96*(column-1),22+96*(row-1).
CivilY0=S. The schematic min Y is S-7, NOT the ground walk level. SI01 is a
separate entry component: use the matching Steam Works/EV connection guide;
do not assume its stored zero WorldEdit origin is your surveyed city origin.

These are the preserved refined shell files, renamed for clarity. Floors still
include the architectural pavers. Follow the separate exact removal/riser patch
only when installing utilities by hand. The machine/utility coordinate guide
and its inventory bill are separate from this architecture-only pack.

The architecture-stock folder includes matching ordinary/cut-part inventory,
coordinate lists and host-grid ZIPs. Keep those architectural counts separate
from the machine/utility item guide. Volume/batch figures are not verified
crafting ingredient bills.

The manifest and static NBT audit prove contents; they do not certify runtime
recipes, ghost render compatibility, game placement or a completed factory.
